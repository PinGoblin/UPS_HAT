# Shipping, storage and disposal

**As supplied:** the board ships without a battery in an anti-static bag. It contains no lithium cells, so it is not classed as dangerous goods and can be sent by standard post or air freight.

**Shipping a unit with a battery fitted** (for example, returning a complete system): lithium-ion batteries are regulated dangerous goods (UN3480 for batteries alone, UN3481 when packed with or fitted in equipment).

- Remove the cells from their holders before packing, and pack them separately in individual insulating sleeves or cases.
- For air transport, discharge the battery to 30 % charge or less.
- Pack the battery so it cannot move, be crushed or short-circuit.
- Check your carrier's rules before sending. Many postal services accept only batteries fitted in equipment, or ban them from air mail entirely.

**Storage**

- Store the boards in their anti-static bags in a dry place between −20 °C and 60 °C.
- For storage longer than a week, shut down the Pi on battery so the HAT enters ship mode, then switch the pack OFF. Switched on, the pack's temperature monitor draws about 0.5 mA (about 12 mAh a day).
- For storage longer than a month, charge the battery to about 50 % first, or remove the cells.
- Store batteries between 10 °C and 25 °C, away from flammable materials, and check their charge every 3 months.

**Disposal:** do not put the board or battery in household waste. Take electronic waste and lithium batteries to an approved recycling point. Tape over the battery terminals before recycling.
