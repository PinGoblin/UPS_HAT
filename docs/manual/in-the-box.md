# What's in the box

**Included**

- 1 × UPS HAT+ board (revision D), with 40-pin stacking header fitted
- 1 × UPS Pack board (DUAL or QUAD, as ordered) with its XT30 lead and gap pads fitted; no cells
- 1 × Quick-start card with a link to this manual and the software

**Not included (purchase separately)**

- Raspberry Pi, microSD card and case
- Lithium-ion cells: two matched unprotected 18650 cells for the DUAL pack, or four for the QUAD (see [Battery requirements](battery.md))
- USB-C PD power adapter. Required: USB Power Delivery at 9 V or 15 V, 3 A (27 W or more); see [Power sources](power-sources.md)
- M2.5 standoffs, screws and nuts for mounting

The board is shipped without a battery, so it can be sent by standard post and air freight.
